# Decommission, create or repurpose a team
Arguments: `decommission <team>`, `create <slug> "<aim>"` or `repurpose <team> "<new work>"`. A team is a hat worn by configured identities, never a separate in-world agent; its members keep their registered names, their keys (`.secrets/`, never opened) and their blocks. Ask the owner one batch of questions with defaults, then do the whole job in one branch.

**Where a team lives** (every procedure walks this list): `teams/<slug>.yaml` (schema `schemas/team.json`: slug, name, aim, story, faction, pipeline, members, home, territory, border, social_budget, links); `agents/<member>/persona.md` (`team:`, knobs, the bible) and `metrics.yaml`; `config/agents.yaml` (identity, team, roles, runtime, `enabled`); `config/local.yaml: agents` (what the supervisor schedules here); `.github/workflows/agent-<slug>.yml` (off while `HG_ENABLED=false`); `teams/blueprints/*.yaml: team` and `pipeline.blueprint`/`next_blueprint` chains in every team file; `teams/exchange.yaml: claims.exclude` lots; `kb/goals`, `kb/INDEX.md`; `reports/examples/**` (byte-compared by `tests/test_pulse.py`); the `AGENTS.md` paragraph; tests (`tests/test_crew.py` names the crew teams, `tests/test_help.py` the helpers); the in-world faction (`found_faction`, `invite`, `leave_faction`) and the public profile line.

1. **Decommission.** Nothing in the world is deleted: our members' blocks and structures stand (the world crumbles an owner's blocks after seven days unseen, so keep every identity's `keepalive` if its builds should stay). Steps: stop scheduling (`config/local.yaml: agents[].enabled: false` for each member; `hglab local status` confirms), mark `enabled: false` in `config/agents.yaml`, set the team's `pipeline` aside (move its open designs to another team's `next_blueprint` so `hglab.help` and the chain carry them), remove the team from other teams' `faction.allies` and `supply` knobs, set `territory.status: proposed` or remove the border signs' duty, say so once in the open in the team founder's voice (one honest line through `hg-be-agent`, within the say budget), update the kb goal to `status: done` or `abandoned` with the evidence, drop the AGENTS.md sentences that gave it standing work, regenerate `reports/examples` if a metric changed, and keep the persona files (a decommissioned team's bibles are the record). Never free a lot, demolish, or hand goods away without the owner's word; `give` into another team's store is the only transfer.
2. **Create.** One question batch: the aim and story in our words, the members (new personas through `hg-new-agent` and `hglab persona new --answers`, or existing ones moving teams), the faction (name, motto, membership, founder), the ground (`hglab team set-territory`, proposed until the owner confirms; a border of signs only when the story wants one), the pipeline (site, radius, range, floor, stock, plan, and a blueprint from `hg-blueprint-design`), the say budget, and influence links `{from, signal, to, effect, plain_english}`. Write `teams/<slug>.yaml`, set each member's `team:`, add the members to `config/agents.yaml` (disabled) and `config/local.yaml` (disabled until the owner enables live play), file the kb goal, add the team's sentence to AGENTS.md, extend `tests/test_crew.py`'s team set when it has crew roles, and run `hglab persona validate`, `hglab config validate`, `hglab sync`, the suite. Registration (`hglab register`) and enabling are the owner's live steps: offer, never perform.
3. **Repurpose.** Keep the characters, change the work: rewrite `aim`/`story` and the pipeline (`blueprint`, `next_blueprint`, `builders`, `stock`, `range`, `focus`, `roam`, or the office's `claims.*` switches such as `walls.enabled`), give members the crew roles the new work needs (`knobs.crew.role`), re-point `metrics.yaml` key results at the new work and bump `version` (then `hglab pulse --all --fixtures --out reports/examples --today 2026-10-17`), update influence links whose signals no longer exist, move or add the team to other teams' `next_blueprint` chains and the office's `exclude`, write the owner's instruction as a dated sentence in AGENTS.md and a kb goal with a ledger-answerable `success_test`, and add tests for the new behaviour while keeping the old mechanism's tests on a helper that restores the old config (as `tests/conftest.py: office_team()` does for the walls). Check `schemas/team.json` and `schemas/persona.json` before adding a key.
4. **Hand-off and watch.** The live supervisor runs the working tree: a saved team file acts at the members' next wakes, and a half-edited one fails their jobs (the territory note has a 300-character cap; pipeline keys must be in the schema). Edit whole files, validate, then watch `runs/local/supervisor.log` for `exit 1` and `hglab local goals` for the new assignments after one adaptation cycle. Record the change in the PR, the kb and the owner's memory with the date.
Check: every file in the list above was opened and either changed or found not to need it; no persona bible lost; no key, variable or secret touched; nothing registered, enabled, demolished or given live without the owner; validators, sync and the suite green before the PR.
Example: 2026-11-03: the land office was repurposed from walling its lots to building the larger New York: `claims.walls.enabled: false`, `blueprint: midtown` with `next_blueprint` and `focus: blueprint`, every member in `builders`, the deed gate waived for the office's own lots in `factory.lands_own`, the wall tests kept on `office_team(walls=True)`, a kb goal and an AGENTS.md sentence; LandBroker placed Grand Central's first steps within the hour.
