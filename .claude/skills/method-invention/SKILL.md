# Invent a method
1. Name the evidenced gap and at least two established baselines.
2. Specify mechanism, assumptions, and exact procedure another agent can follow.
3. Preregister a metric and trial against the baselines.
4. Score effectiveness, energy/token/time cost, generality, robustness, and simplicity.
5. Adopt only after P3 replication; turn adopted methods into skills.
CLI: `hglab kb new method "TITLE"`; validate with `hglab kb validate`.
Check: statuses are proposed, trialed, adopted, or retired.
Example: compare event replay with periodic snapshots for exact state reconstruction.
