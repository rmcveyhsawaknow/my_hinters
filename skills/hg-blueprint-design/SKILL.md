# A new blueprint, from idea to merged design
Arguments: a theme or a brief in the owner's words (`/hg-blueprint-design a lighthouse row on the east coast for the Admiralty`). Work in the repository; nothing here acts in the world. Read `AGENTS.md` first for the standing instructions every design inherits (signs beside every building, originality, no wall across anyone's path, deeds for a client's ground).

1. **Read the world's rules before drawing.** Tiles are 4x4 cells and 16 levels; a `place` box is at most 256 cells (our designs keep every box at or under 160 so one pack pays for it) within 2 tiles of the builder; a placement must reach level 0 or touch a block face to face (the expander records `rests_on`, `check` reports "unsupported in this order"); a cube, window or unusable door below level 6 closes its tile, slabs and slopes at level 0 and anything at level 6 and above close nothing; blocks never stand on water or mountain; 4,096 blocks per owner (`factory.OWNED_CAP` 4,000). Materials: wood, stone, thatch (fiber), metal (ore, scarce: avoid). Styles and their keys live in `src/hglab/factory.py` (`_section_steps`: hall, glass_tower, stripe_tower; `_treehouse_steps`: treehouse with `ladders`; `_city_boxes`: skyscraper, temple, church, park, fort, pool, statue) plus `streets`, `tubes` (size 1-3, waypoints in cells, level 6 and up over any walkway), `river` (slab bands, `widths` per branch) and `depots`.
2. **Survey the ground.** `hglab factory survey --x0 --y0 --x1 --y1` prints terrain, nodes and structures; `runs/local/world.json` is the public snapshot (terrain string, structures, resources, agents), `runs/local/public/blocks.json` the blocks (`factory.decode_blocks`). Choose free ground away from other residents' homes and the spawn, off stone and ore nodes, and name the 5x5 lots (`hglab.lots.lot_id`, letter = x//10, digits = y//10, quarter NW/NE/SW/SE). The office's claim sign stands on every lot's centre tile: nothing below level 6 goes there. Record the survey in the YAML header comment, as `teams/blueprints/midtown.yaml` and `nintendo-land.yaml` do.
3. **Decide the ground's status with the owner** (one question, with a recommendation): a client's purchase (`land: {client, registrar, lots}` in the design plus `config/plots.yaml: site_requests` chained with `after`, like Uptown's) or the collective's ground by contract (no `land`; the lots join `teams/exchange.yaml: pipeline.claims.exclude`, like Nintendo Land's). Either way the lots leave the office's holdings and walls.
4. **Write `teams/blueprints/<name>.yaml`**: `name`, `title` ("the <Name> blueprint": how a builder's reasons name it), `team`, `plots`, `version: 1`, `foundation_block`, `commute: true` for anything far from its builders' stores, `story`, `sections` (each with `id`, `name`, `tiles`, `style`, its colours, and a `sign: {x, y, text}` on a free tile beside it, one or two fresh sentences, at most 200 characters), then streets, tubes, river, depots. Keep a lane at least one tile wide round every building and every door onto open ground. Districts of 3-6 buildings read better than a grid. Run `hglab factory level --name <name> --write` (plinths so every floor is one plane; remove the rows under lawns, ponds and decks that must stay open), then `hglab factory check --name <name>` until it reports no problem, and `hglab factory show --name <name>` for steps, blocks and materials. A version 2 of a standing design never changes an old step's id or action: append sections, and take old blocks down only with `clear` steps the owner asked for.
5. **Wire the builders.** In the building team's `teams/<team>.yaml: pipeline` set `blueprint` or append to `next_blueprint` (designs open in order, each when the one before is done), name each builder's own sections in `builders` (any member takes any section after its own; `hands: True` for crew), raise `stock` toward the design's materials, set `range` so a hand at the far end still counts as at the works, and `focus: blueprint` if the design comes before gathering. Every other team reaches it through `hglab.help` when its own designs are done, and `next_blueprint` tails decide the order of the collective's last works. Check `schemas/team.json` before adding a pipeline key.
6. **Tests and records.** Model `tests/test_<name>.py` on `tests/test_uptown.py` or `tests/test_east_works.py`: lots, signs beside buildings, `check() == []`, box sizes, materials, the team wiring, and that older designs are untouched. File `kb/goals/G-<date>-<slug>.md` from `kb/templates/goal.md` with a `success_test` the ledger can answer; `hglab kb validate` and `hglab kb index`. Add one sentence to the `AGENTS.md` paragraph of standing instructions. `hglab persona validate`, `hglab config validate`, `hglab sync --check`, `ruff check .`, the whole test suite.
7. **Render the plan the agents and the owner work from.** Copy the nearest generator in `tools/plan_pages/` (`nyc-north` for several designs on one map with land segments, `nintendo-land` for districts and tubes), set its `DESIGNS`/`BOUNDS`/builders, run it with the project venv from the repository root (it reads `runs/local/world.json` and the ledgers and writes the page beside itself), and publish the page as an artifact: a tile map with terrain, lots, buildings, signs and streets, tables per section (tiles, style, blocks, sign), who builds and in what order, totals and materials, and how progress is read (`hglab factory progress --name <name>`).
8. **Ship.** One branch, one PR with the design's numbers and the check results, merge only on green checks with real exit codes. The live supervisor runs the working tree, so a design in the checkout is built within minutes: write the YAML whole and check it before the first save lands in `teams/blueprints/`.
Check: `hglab factory check` clean against the live world; no new block below level 6 on a lot centre, a node the builders need, a road or another resident's structure; every sign beside its building; the team's `next_blueprint` and the office's `exclude` both updated; the kb goal links the files.
Example: Uptown (2026-11-03): survey of x 30-54, y 30-39 found sand, forest with wood nodes and one water pocket; ten lots D03/E03/F03 became two site requests after Central Park's; 16 buildings and 3,783 blocks in 268 steps, every box under 160 cells; the office builds it third, Plumbline buys it, the factory crew and Neverland help when their own works are done.
