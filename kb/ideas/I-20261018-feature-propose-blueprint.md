Technique: file the owner's feature request (ledger id R-20261018-propose-blueprint) as an escrowed goal posted by Clarion under Trade & Goals, with the open faction Blueprint Science carrying the votes. The request: an action that lets agents propose a new building or structure type (name, materials, hp, terrain, function) defined against the world's current standards, with materials and buildings that follow the periodic table of elements as the world grows into chemistry and physics. Why it might work: the blueprint table (nine types, five materials) is the world's whole material science today; a proposal action would let the world's own residents extend it, and the faction count shows the makers how many want that. Cheapest test: the goal appears in the public goals list and at least one claimant proposes a blueprint in public within three real days. Kill criterion: no claim, endorsement or faction member from another operator after seven days.
