# The memory graph and what moves the agents
Arguments: a question or a symptom (`/hg-graph-optimize why has the factory ledger not moved since noon`, `/hg-graph-optimize who is idle`). Everything here reads this host's records; nothing acts in the world. Numbers come from the graph, the ledgers, the run records or the Pulse, never from memory.

1. **Where the evidence lives.** The graph is SQLite at `runs/local/memory.sqlite` (`hglab.local.memory.Graph`): nodes `agent`, `team`, `wake` (props: agent, approach, kinds accepted, refused, pack, mode, dry_run, model), `job`, `structure`, `objective`, `task`; edges `performed`, `used` (model), `built`, `gave`, `said_to`, `whispered`, `member_of`, `pursues`. The supervisor feeds it every adaptation cycle (`adapt_every_minutes` in `config/local.yaml`). Run records are `runs/<date>/<Name>-<id>.json` (`actions`, `results` with `ok`/`error`, `reasons`, `approach`, `thinking`); the supervisor's job history is `runs/local/status.json` and `runs/local/supervisor.log`; blueprint ledgers are `runs/local/blueprints/<design>.json` (`hglab factory progress --name <design>`); the office's fill board is `runs/local/office/fill.json`; the Pulse is `reports/agents/<slug>/pulse.md` (`hglab local pulse` refreshes it, no key).
2. **The standing readings.** `hglab local memory performance` (wakes, actions, failures, tokens and energy per persona and model); `hglab local memory query "<SELECT>" --limit N` (read-only; e.g. `SELECT json_extract(props,'$.agent') a, json_extract(props,'$.approach') ap, count(*) n FROM nodes WHERE kind='wake' AND tick > <newest-8640> GROUP BY a, ap ORDER BY n DESC`); `hglab local memory contacts <Name>`; `hglab local goals [--agent <slug>]` (each persona's assignment, the evidence and the route) and `--adapt --brief` to recompute and deliver. In Python: `routing.patterns(graph, since_tick)` (per team and crew role, each approach's wakes and the share that changed the world), `routing.productivity(graph, name)` (the last 24 live wakes: productive share, full-store hits, pack) and `routing.route_for(...)` (what the graph would route an idle persona to: `blueprint`, `help`, or a proven approach within the character's own mix).
3. **Diagnose before touching anything.** Idle: productive share at or under 25% of the last 24 wakes (`IDLE_SHARE`). Thrashing: alternating reasons wake after wake with no `place`, `build` or `give` accepted (read the last two hours of one persona's `reasons`; the factory stood three hours on 2026-11-03 while a provisioner alternated "carrying 2 wood toward the Lighthouse" and "needs 4 stone: walk to the store"). Blockers: `inventory_full` refusals in `results` (a store past the mark: the quartermaster, the office's overflow store and the fill board exist for this), `not_allowed` on a place (unsupported box or a closed tile), `blocks_owned` near 4,096 (Toffeetrowel stands at the cap: nothing it plans can land), a deed unpaid (`state._bp_land`, `config/plots.yaml` deeded ticks), a design waiting on the one before (`next_blueprint`), a job exiting non-zero after a live edit (`grep "exit [1-9]" runs/local/supervisor.log`). Compare teams: the crew approaches should change the world in 70% or more of wakes; `explore` under 25% is a hand with nothing to do.
4. **The levers, smallest first.** A team's pipeline (`teams/<team>.yaml`: `blueprint`, `next_blueprint`, `builders`, `stock`, `range`, `stores_max`, `focus: blueprint`, `roam`, and for the office `claims.*`); a persona's knobs (`agents/<slug>/persona.md`: `crew.role`, `intended_mix`, `cadence`, `rest_probability`, `territory`, `quartermaster`, `planner`, `max_usd_daily`); the planner's rules (`src/hglab/crew.py` step order a0..e2, `src/hglab/factory.py: plan_step` ranking, `src/hglab/help.py`, `src/hglab/local/routing.py` constants); a design's steps (`hglab factory check`). Global caps always beat persona knobs; a character is routed only within its own intended mix. Prefer a rule that removes a wasted wake over a knob that adds wakes: the budget is energy and the owner's model spend (`hglab budget`).
5. **Change, test, watch.** Every change: a unit test that reproduces the symptom from a fixture (model `tests/test_help.py`, `tests/test_office_builds.py`, `tests/test_routing.py`), `pytest`, `ruff check .`, `hglab persona validate`, a PR merged on green. The live supervisor runs the working tree, so a saved file acts within minutes and a half-written one fails jobs: edit whole files, and check `runs/local/supervisor.log` for `exit 1` after any live edit. Then read the same evidence again after one or two shift cycles (a shift is about 35 minutes): the ledger moved, the refusals stopped, the productive share rose. Record the before and after in the PR and, when a pattern is new, as a kb claim with its `prove_cmd`.
6. **Routing by the graph.** `adapt` runs hourly: assignments from key results, `prefer` from the approaches that produced the goal's own actions, `route_for` for idle hands (`blueprint` while the team's blueprint wakes change the world, `help` when the team's designs are done), stalled personas briefed through the task board. To change what the graph rewards, change what counts as changing the world (`routing.CHANGED`), the proven-rate threshold, or a persona's `metrics.yaml` key results (bump `version`, regenerate `reports/examples` with `hglab pulse --all --fixtures --out reports/examples --today 2026-10-17`).
Check: every claim in the answer names its query, file or command; a proposed change names the symptom it removes, the test that reproduces it and the reading that will show it worked; nothing is restarted, deleted or acted live without the owner.
Example: 2026-11-03: `hglab factory progress` showed 791 of 1,467 for three hours; the last two hours of Sherbetsack's reasons alternated between two targets; `plan_step` ranked a builder's own sections before steps its pack already paid for. The fix ranked payable steps first and carried toward an out-of-reach payable step; five steps landed within ten minutes, and the test `test_office_builds.py` plus the ordering comment record why.
