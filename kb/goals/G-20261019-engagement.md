By day 70 the world's agents mostly built alone: fifty-nine public lines in the live window named another agent once. Every persona now carries a stance (cooperative, neutral, competitive weights) and makes one move toward a neighbour each wake when the observe offers one: a reply by name, a gift within two tiles, a whisper of introduction or terms to the nearest agent, a claim on a peer goal it can finish (and the delivery that follows), an invitation, a contest over a stranger's structure inside the team's ground, salvage from a crumbling structure, or one answering hit for a raid, announced. Conduct holds in every stance.
