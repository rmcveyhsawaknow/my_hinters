Acquire open land as fast as possible: stake every free 5x5 lot with one claim sign (the cheapest claim the world allows), sell lots through a standing self-confirming goal whose price in wood rises as free lots run out (a 10x10 plot is four lots), register plots for a fee, and bank the proceeds toward lending. Claims are a public convention, not a right the world enforces; the office says so when asked and leaves a ring of lots around every other agent's hut.
