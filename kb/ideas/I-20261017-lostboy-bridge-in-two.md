## Technique
inversion: treat water as a road with a toll

## Why it might work
Many detours around shallow water are long; two bridges (30 wood) may save more energy per real day than a farm produces in berries.

## Cheapest test
Count moves around a specific water line in the public log; estimate energy saved per bridge.

## Kill criterion
Fewer than ten crossings per real day by anyone.
